### 🧩 The problem
#### Sales teams kept hearing the same disqualifier: "Do you support nomination workflows?" Keka's award programs could only do direct winner selection — we were out of the room before the comparison started.

We redesigned award programs to close that gap: structured nominations, a review chain, and richer category setup.
